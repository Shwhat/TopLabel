# Fonts

TopLabel has 33 font families built in. They are free to use under the SIL Open Font License and are stored with the program, so they work without the internet. Library, Fonts lists them with their styles.

| Group | Fonts |
|---|---|
| Casual | Comic Neue, Comic Relief, Patrick Hand, Short Stack, Gochi Hand, Kalam |
| Sans | Roboto, Open Sans, Lato, Montserrat, Poppins, Nunito, Inter, Source Sans 3, Work Sans, Rubik |
| Condensed and display | Oswald, Bebas Neue, Anton, Barlow Condensed, Archivo Black |
| Fantasy and serif | Cinzel, Cinzel Decorative, MedievalSharp, Uncial Antiqua, IM Fell English, Cormorant Garamond, Merriweather, Spectral |
| Rounded and technical | Fredoka, Varela Round, Quicksand, Roboto Mono |

The default is Comic Neue Bold.

## Styles

Each family has a regular style and, where the family offers them, bold, italic and bold italic. TopLabel uses the style that matches Bold and Italic in the Properties panel. When a family has no bold, the regular letters are thickened slightly; when it has no italic, the letters are slanted.

## Characters

The fonts cover the Latin alphabet with accents, digits and common punctuation. Other characters print as an empty box.
